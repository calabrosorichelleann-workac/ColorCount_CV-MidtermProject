Screenshots captured while colorcount_cv.py is running (press S to save).
Pressing S saves detection_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.png in this folder.
If the mask window is open (press M), it also saves masks_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.png.

Used in docs/ColorCount_Documentation.pdf:
  Figure 1 - detection_one_object.png       (one object detected)
  Figure 2 - detection_multiple_colors.png  (several colors, per-color counts)
  Figure 3 - test_limitation.png            (dim light or touching objects)

Rename the saved files to the names above, then insert them into the paper.
